# GRADUATE TRACER STUDY SURVEY

**Alumni Survey Questionnaire — Pasig Education Impact Index (PEII)**

**Target respondents:** All Alumni
**Format:** Two-phase. The original 64 numbered questions are retained below. The university export adds a Barangay question and a post-graduation employment section; its 30 industry-category columns are represented as one branched question.

**Purpose:** This survey aims to assess the outcomes of graduates from Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Pasig (PLP) and determine how their education has contributed to their employment, financial stability, personal development, and community engagement. The results will be used to compute the Pasig Education Impact Index (PEII) and to support the continuous improvement of educational programs and policies.

**Instructions:** Please answer the following questions honestly and completely. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).

The original PEII questions are required. For the added questions, choices are observed in the university response export except for the official Pasig barangay list, industry categories explicitly marked **Proposed**, and role choices labeled **Proposed addition**. These are not verified as the university's exact form options. The export does not establish their requiredness or original input types. Questions without options in the original numbered questionnaire are open text responses. Before the form, respondents review the server-owned Data Privacy Statement and the consent statement below.

Every PEII impact statement uses the same 5-point scale:

| Score | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Strongly Disagree |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 4 | Agree |
| 5 | Strongly Agree |

The questionnaire is answered in two phases:

* **Phase 1 — Baseline (Before):** consent, respondent profile (including the conditional Barangay question), the five II-A PEII impact sections, and feedback.
* **Phase 2 — Follow-up (After):** current/post-graduation employment questions and the five II-B PEII impact sections, which repeat the Phase 1 statements to capture the post-graduation outcome.

The university workbook is a single-sheet export containing both BEFORE and AFTER answers; it does not identify PEII's phases. The placement of supplemental fields below follows the two-phase PEII design and should be confirmed against the university's source form before being treated as its exact routing.

## Definition Sources

The added questions and observed choices above were reconciled against `2023 - 2025 Respondents as of 15 Sep 2026.xlsx`, sheet `Form Responses 1`; the complete barangay choices come from the Philippine Statistics Authority, not the export. The export's 43 additional columns map to one Barangay question, 12 general employment questions, and 30 conditional industry-category fields represented here as one branched question. Proposed category choices in the nine unobserved and 18 sparsely observed branches, and the proposed additions to the unified role list, are separate from that response evidence and need confirmation against the university's source form before deployment. The two generated PEII definitions below now include these supplemental questions for newly generated surveys. The saved template is synchronized when opened for editing or generation, while existing non-template surveys are not changed automatically. The export also does not contain the original questionnaire's name, email, and contact-number columns in `Form Responses 1`, so their absence should not be interpreted as proof that the university did not ask them.

* `frontend/src/components/survey-management/constants.ts` and `graduate-tracer-options.ts` — the definition and choice lists used by **Generate Questionnaire** in the survey management UI. Opening the template editor or generation preview synchronizes an outdated saved template to this definition; preview, editing, and generation then use that saved template. Existing non-template surveys are not retroactively changed.
* `backend/scripts/seed_alumni_questionnaire.py` — the CLI seed that creates the same questionnaire for backend environments. Both definitions include the 15 **Degree Program Category** options and the supplemental fields above.

The industry-category choices are conditional on the selected industry, and the role list is shared across industries. The additional short-answer fields appear only after their matching **Other** choice is selected.
